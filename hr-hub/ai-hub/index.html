Before you begin

Assessment instructions

Read these rules carefully. The timer starts only after you click Start timed assessment.

01
20 questions that get harder as you go

Scenarios are drawn from a reviewed bank and mixed with freshly generated calculations, so no two attempts are the same. Difficulty starts at your experience level and rises towards the end.

02
Single best response

Every option is written to be plausible. Choose the one that is strongest once evidence, risk, people impact, compliance and business outcome are weighed together. Nothing in the wording or length of an option gives the answer away.

03
Strict timed session

Your time limit will appear here.

04
Maximum 3 pauses

Each pause freezes the timer. While paused, the exam is locked until you explicitly continue.

05
Automatic submission

When the timer reaches 00:00, the assessment ends automatically. Unanswered questions are recorded as unanswered and score zero.

06
Assessment integrity

For a meaningful result, do not use AI, web search, colleagues or answer-pattern guessing. A basic calculator or scratchpad may be used for numerical payroll questions.

07
You may review answers

Use Previous and Next while time remains, and change any answer before you submit. Keyboard: A–D to choose, Enter for next.

08
Protected exam window

Clicking outside the assessment will not close it. Use only the × button; closing an active session will require confirmation and the attempt will be lost.

02 / HOW THE HUB THINKS

AI output should
support judgement.

Brainstorming is separated from verified rules. Current law, policy and company-specific facts still need validation before action.

Facts before output

Structured inputs reduce guessing and force the scenario to include the facts that matter.

Explain the logic

Payroll and decision tools surface assumptions, checks and risks instead of giving an unexplained answer.

Human review stays essential

Generated analysis and drafts are working material, not a substitute for current official guidance or employer policy.

HR × AI

Better questions.
Better HR thinking.

Structure complex work, challenge assumptions and turn HR knowledge into practical next steps.

Emiratisation is now a standing part of workforce planning for UAE private-sector companies. For 2026, the targets follow the same half-yearly rhythm as recent years. With the year-end deadline approaching, HR teams need to know exactly where they stand.

## Who the targets apply to

The half-yearly targets apply to **private-sector companies with 50 or more employees** that are subject to MOHRE's Emiratisation policies. They focus on increasing the share of UAE nationals in **skilled roles**.

## The 2026 targets and deadlines

* The target is a **2% increase** in skilled Emirati employees over the year.
* It is split into **1% for the first half** and **1% for the second half**.
* The first-half deadline was **30 June 2026**.
* The second-half target needs to be achieved by **31 December 2026**.

## What happens if a company misses the target

From 1 July 2026, MOHRE began applying financial contributions to companies that missed their first-half target. The contribution is **AED 10,000 per month (AED 120,000 a year) for each required Emirati position that was not filled**.

MOHRE has also made clear that "fake Emiratisation", such as appointing nationals to roles that don't exist or that they don't actually perform, is treated as a serious violation. The ministry uses monitoring systems to detect these practices.

## The upside of meeting targets

Companies with strong Emiratisation results can join the **Emiratisation Partners Club**. It offers discounts of up to 80% on MOHRE service fees, along with other priority benefits.

## How HR can plan well

1. **Know your number early.** Calculate your skilled headcount and the exact number of additional Emirati hires each target requires. Don't wait for the final month.
2. **Hire into real, skilled roles.** Targets count skilled positions. Map roles where Emiratis can build careers, not just fill a quota.
3. **Use Nafis.** MOHRE encourages employers to use the Nafis platform to connect with Emirati jobseekers across specialisations.
4. **Complete registrations properly.** A hire only helps if the contract, MOHRE registration, pension registration and contributions are all in order.
5. **Build retention, not just hiring.** A target met in June can be lost by December if new hires leave. Onboarding, mentoring and clear career paths matter.
6. **Track monthly.** Treat Emiratisation like any other KPI, with an owner and a monthly review.

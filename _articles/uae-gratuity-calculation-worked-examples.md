End-of-service gratuity is one of the most frequently miscalculated payments in UAE payroll. The rule itself is short, but part-years, unpaid leave and the cap catch people out. This guide walks through the method for private-sector employees under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, with worked examples.

## The rules in brief

* **Eligibility:** at least **one year** of continuous service.
* **First five years:** **21 days' basic wage** for each year of service.
* **After five years:** **30 days' basic wage** for each additional year.
* **Part-years:** count **pro rata**.
* **Unpaid absence:** days of unpaid leave are **not counted** in the service period.
* **Cap:** total gratuity cannot exceed **two years' wage**.
* **Basis:** the **last basic wage**, not total salary with allowances.
* **Timing:** end-of-service dues should be paid within **14 days** of the contract ending.
* **Resignation:** under the current law, resigning does not reduce gratuity the way it did under the old law.

## The daily rate

Gratuity is expressed in days, so you need a daily basic wage. Many employers and calculators use **basic ÷ 30**. Whatever method you use, apply it consistently and make sure it matches your policy and current guidance.

## Example 1: under five years

* Basic wage: AED 9,000
* Service: 3 years and 6 months (3.5 years)
* Daily rate: 9,000 ÷ 30 = AED 300

Gratuity = 21 × 3.5 × 300 = **AED 22,050**

## Example 2: more than five years

* Basic wage: AED 15,000
* Service: 7 years and 3 months (7.25 years)
* Daily rate: 15,000 ÷ 30 = AED 500

* First five years: 21 × 5 = 105 days
* Remaining 2.25 years: 30 × 2.25 = 67.5 days
* Total: 172.5 days × 500 = **AED 86,250**

A common mistake is applying 30 days to the whole service period, which would give AED 108,750. That overpays by AED 22,500.

## Example 3: with unpaid leave

* Basic wage: AED 12,000
* Total time with the employer: 6 years and 6 months (6.5 years)
* Unpaid leave during service: 73 days (0.2 years)
* Daily rate: 12,000 ÷ 30 = AED 400

* Eligible service: 6.5 − 0.2 = 6.3 years
* First five years: 105 days
* Remaining 1.3 years: 30 × 1.3 = 39 days
* Total: 144 days × 400 = **AED 57,600**

## Example 4: the two-year cap

The cap matters for very long service. Two years' wage equals 24 months of the relevant wage. Check the total against this limit before you pay. With 21 and 30-day accruals, the cap usually only bites after more than two decades of service.

## Common mistakes to avoid

1. **Using total salary instead of basic.** Allowances are not part of the gratuity basis.
2. **Ignoring part-years.** Months beyond the last full year still count pro rata.
3. **Counting unpaid leave as service.** It must be excluded.
4. **Applying old resignation reductions.** These no longer apply under the current law.
5. **Paying late.** The 14-day deadline applies to end-of-service dues.

## Alternative savings schemes

Since 2023, private-sector employers (including in free zones) have had the option to pay monthly contributions into an approved investment scheme instead of accruing gratuity in the traditional way. If your company has joined such a scheme, the rules for the covered period are different, so check before calculating.
